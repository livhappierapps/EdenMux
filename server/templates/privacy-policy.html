EdenMux
Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026  ·  Last Updated: October 15, 2026

EdenMux is a mobile terminal emulator application. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is handled. We believe in being straightforward — we collect only what is necessary to run the app.

1. Who We Are

EdenMux is an independently developed application created by a solo developer. Questions about this policy can be directed to Oblivionhappier@icloud.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to provide the app's features:

  • Account information — When you register with email and password, we store your email address, an optional username, and a securely hashed version of your password (we never store your password in plain text). When you sign in with Apple, we receive only the anonymous identifier Apple provides — we do not receive your real email unless you choose to share it.
  • Device identifier — A randomly generated device ID is stored locally on your device. This is used solely to maintain your AI memory, moderation status, and session continuity. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Daily message counter — Free-tier users have a limit of 15 Eden messages per day. This counter is stored locally on your device and resets at midnight. It is not sent to our servers.
  • Terminal session data — iOS terminal activity stays in the app. Android and web shell input and output are sent to our server for real-time execution. We do not automatically send terminal activity to Eden or third-party AI services.
  • Messages to Eden — When you send a message to Eden (our AI assistant), that message is sent to our server and forwarded to xAI's Grok API to generate a response. If you have an active Core Unlock, your conversation history is stored on our servers to provide Eden with memory of past interactions. Free-tier messages are not stored after the response is delivered.
  • Voice input — If you use the microphone feature (Core Unlock only), your spoken audio is sent to our server and forwarded to ElevenLabs Scribe for transcription. Audio is not stored after transcription. Microphone access requires your explicit permission.
  • Purchase information — In-app purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details. RevenueCat provides us with purchase status (what you have unlocked) to enable paid features.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, or photos.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not automatically include terminal activity in Eden conversations sent to xAI.
  • We do not store voice audio after transcription.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • To generate responses from Eden (our AI assistant).
  • To enforce our community guidelines and moderation policy.
  • To generate Eden's voice responses via ElevenLabs text-to-speech.
  • To transcribe your voice input via ElevenLabs Scribe (Core Unlock feature).
  • To enable paid features you have unlocked via RevenueCat.
  • To maintain Eden's memory of your past conversations (Core Unlock only).

5. Third-Party Services

EdenMux integrates with the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Apple Sign In — Used for iOS authentication. Apple's privacy policy applies to the sign-in flow. We receive only a unique Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address.
  • xAI (Grok) — Eden's responses are powered by Grok, xAI's large language model. Your messages are transmitted to xAI's API to generate responses. Please review xAI's privacy policy.
  • ElevenLabs — Eden's voice is generated by ElevenLabs text-to-speech. Eden's response text is transmitted to ElevenLabs to generate audio. Audio is streamed to your device and not stored by us. Please review ElevenLabs' privacy policy.
  • ElevenLabs Scribe — Voice-to-text transcription for microphone input (Core Unlock only). Audio is sent to ElevenLabs for transcription and not retained by us afterward. Please review ElevenLabs' privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — Manages in-app purchase entitlements. RevenueCat receives your Apple App Store purchase receipts and provides us with your entitlement status to enable paid features. No payment card data is shared with us. Please review RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Resend — Delivers password-reset emails. Your email address and one-time reset link are sent to Resend for delivery. Please review Resend's privacy policy.

6. Microphone Access

The microphone is used solely for voice input to Eden (Core Unlock feature). When you tap the microphone button, audio is recorded and sent to our server for transcription. The microphone is never accessed in the background. You can revoke microphone permission at any time in your device Settings.

7. Data Storage and Security

Account passwords are stored on our server as bcrypt hashes. Mobile authentication tokens use OS secure storage when available, with app-storage fallback; the web app uses app storage. We use reasonable measures to protect data, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Theme and app preferences are stored locally. On Android and web, shell input and output pass through our server for real-time execution.

8. Data Retention

Your account and associated data are retained for as long as your account is active. Eden conversation memory (Core Unlock) is retained to provide continuity across sessions. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Upon request, we will delete your account credentials, conversation memory, and device moderation record within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

EdenMux is not directed at children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of EdenMux after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
